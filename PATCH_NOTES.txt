NOISE-FLOOR GUARD FIX (plot_accuracy_vs_M.py)
==============================================
One file. Your dim-64 figures showed the guard was too lenient: it required
only 2 points above the SEM, and in the oscillator's noisy wobble exactly two
points poked over by a hair -- so it still printed slopes (M^-0.54, M^-0.33)
that are pure sampling noise.

FIXED RULE: a bias point counts as resolved only if it exceeds 2x the SEM, and
at least THREE such points are required before any slope is quoted; otherwise
the legend reads "below noise floor (upper bound)".

Verified against your actual dim-64 numbers:
  spin energy      6 resolved  -> quotes M^-0.95   (your real convergence law)
  spin coherence   2 resolved  -> upper bound, no slope
  osc energy       1 resolved  -> upper bound, no slope
  osc coherence    2 resolved  -> upper bound, no slope

So the one defensible rate is kept and the three floor-limited ones stop
advertising fake exponents.
